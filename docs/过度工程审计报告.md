### 5.3 高

  `[service/impl/QcBatchServiceImpl.java:215]`（submit 方法，全类 `@Transactional` 计数 = 0）· `[mapper/QcTaskMapper.java:23,32]`（`SELECT GET_LOCK` / `SELECT RELEASE_LOCK`）· `[mapper/DbLockMapper.java:32,43]`
